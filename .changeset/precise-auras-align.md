**Added:** Expanded Aura Manager editing with group selection and per-aura appearance controls.

- **Group editing:** Drag across the aura overlay to select intersecting auras, then move the selected group together without losing its arrangement.
- **Aura controls:** Hide resize handles and configure outlines, shadows, rounded corners, icon shapes, icon offsets, icon zoom up to 200%, or reset an aura to its defaults from organized property tabs.
- **Precise positioning:** Position default, arched, and pointer auras with center-relative X and Y offsets. Zero centers an aura, while negative values move it left or down.
- **Pointer workflow:** Complete a pointer aura with Escape after choosing any supported number of points.
- **Capture visibility:** The Settings toggle now controls whether every Hinekora overlay appears in Windows screenshots, Hinekora recordings and rewinds, and other capture tools.
- **Replay feedback:** Replay status notifications use shorter processing and saved messages while preserving actionable failure details.
